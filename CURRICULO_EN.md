## Selected Projects

### OSC Financing Brazil

Data pipeline and dashboard for analyzing the financing of Civil Society Organizations in Brazil.

The project integrates public datasets, organizes territorial and thematic indicators, and provides an analytical layer for research, public policy analysis, and institutional intelligence.

**Demonstrated competencies:** Python, pandas, Streamlit, Plotly, public data, reproducible pipelines, public policy analysis, and technical documentation.

**Repository:** https://github.com/brauliorrs/osc-financiamento-brasil

---

### Audiovisual Memory Network

Digital observatory designed to map and analyze the public presence of audiovisual archives on platforms, archives, and digital aggregators.

The project connects communication, memory, audiovisual heritage, digital circulation, and platform analysis, with potential applications in academic research, digital preservation, and media ecosystem studies.

**Demonstrated competencies:** Python, Streamlit, responsible scraping, digital archive analysis, applied research, methodological organization, and scientific documentation.

**Repository:** https://github.com/brauliorrs/memoria-audiovisual-rede

---

### Fomenta Pesquisa

Automated system for monitoring research grants, funding opportunities, and public calls.

The project collects opportunities from public sources, organizes data, generates an editorial queue, and supports social media publishing workflows, connecting automation, scientific research, and institutional communication.

**Demonstrated competencies:** Python, GitHub Actions, editorial automation, APIs, card generation, monitoring of public sources, and assisted publishing.

**Repository:** https://github.com/brauliorrs/fomenta-pesquisa

---

### Educational Observatory of Paraíba

Project for analyzing demand for higher education in Paraíba based on SiSU data, with a focus on municipalities where IFPB has institutional presence.

The project supports studies on interiorization, educational mobility, territorial alignment, and institutional planning.

**Demonstrated competencies:** Python, pandas, Streamlit, educational analysis, data visualization, public data, and institutional planning.

**Repository:** https://github.com/brauliorrs/observatorio-educacional-paraiba

---

### Tripset — Case Study

Digital product for film tourism that connects films, series, and real destinations.

The project combines audiovisual curation, film and series data, tourist destinations, affiliate links, SEO, and future content automation.

**Demonstrated competencies:** Next.js, Vercel, TypeScript, digital product development, travel tech, editorial curation, affiliate strategy, and monetization.

**Public case study:** https://github.com/brauliorrs/tripset-case-study

---

### Submeta Aí — Case Study

AI-based product designed to support researchers before submitting scientific articles.

The proposal involves journal suitability analysis, author guidelines reading, scope evaluation, publication pattern analysis, and improvement recommendations before submission.

**Demonstrated competencies:** applied AI, academic SaaS, APIs, editorial analysis, freemium model, privacy, digital product development, and scientific communication.
